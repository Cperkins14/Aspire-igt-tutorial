How the simulation works

This tool sends your patient profile to Llama 3 (running locally on your Mac) and asks it to play 100 rounds of the Iowa Gambling Task while staying in character. Here's the exact sequence for each trial:

  1. The tool builds a prompt with your profile + current trial number + current balance + recent choices
  2. It sends the prompt to Llama and asks it to respond with only A, B, C, or D
  3. The tool applies the real IGT reward/penalty structure for that deck
  4. It records the choice, updates the balance, and moves to the next trial
  5. After 100 trials, one complete run is saved to your results

What Ollama needs to be running

Make sure the Ollama app is open on your Mac (look for the llama icon in your menu bar). If you see a red connection status at the top, click the Retry button or open Ollama from your Applications folder.
